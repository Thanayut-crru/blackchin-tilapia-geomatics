# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-13

Peer-review revision (round 2). No result reported in version 1.1.0 changed.

- Added `revision_round2/` with the scripts and outputs for Tables S3-S10 of the revised article:
  - S3: record-level provenance and photographs of the 38 records, re-queried on 13 November 2026;
  - S4: rank stability of held-out permutation importance;
  - S5: leave-one-repetition-out check of configuration selection;
  - S6-S7: comparison of pseudo-absence strategies (matched-pair and partition-level tests);
  - S8: 12-setting sensitivity analysis of the uncertainty-zone interval and buffer, with fallback logging;
  - S9: Moran's I correlogram of presence residuals;
  - S10: sensitivity to 14 grid cells omitted because duplicate cells were removed before records after 2024
    were excluded.
- The uncertainty-zone baseline re-run reproduces the archived RF/M_SAP/uncertainty-zone results exactly
  (`revision_round2/tables/TableS8_reproduction_check.csv`).
- Added the inputs used by these scripts: `audit/output_audit/occurrence_clean.csv`,
  `audit/original_download/occurrences_thailand.csv` (original GBIF download of Thailand records, 13 June 2026),
  and `audit/blackchin_audit_verified.xlsx` (GBIF/iNaturalist re-query of 13 November 2026).
- Extended `verify_release.py` with checks of the round-2 values reported in the revised article.
- Documentation: the third algorithm is the elastic-net hinge logistic model (EN-Hinge), labelled `MXN` in code
  and output files; two unused constants in `s00_config.py` are now commented as legacy.
- Removed `blackchin_tilapia_analysis/output/sensitivity/` and `paper_tables/tableE_sensitivity.csv`, which came
  from an earlier 22-record exploratory run and are not used by the article (they remain in version 1.1.0).
- Known issue retained for traceability: in `revision_outputs/occurrence_model_records.csv`, the
  `country_code` and `state_province` fields of the nine records obtained directly from iNaturalist contain
  locality text. The analysis does not use these fields; Table S3 gives the province of every record.
